# The Earldom of Ek

_Example hex crawl from the Mausritter rulebook._

## Rumours (d6)

1. Blackmore is kidnapping mice to work in her lair
2. Merchants are bringing mice to town and selling them as food
3. The Earl of Ek has fallen ill, and now his heir has taken control
4. A wolf den at Blind Man's Bluff is attracting a pack of wolves
5. Twelve have a cache in the heart of the old forest
6. The Cult of Sage know the secret of everything

## Encounters (d6)

1. d6 Bandits: looking to rob a mark
2. d6 Beetles: dropping a sack of stolen goods
3. d6 Deeritz, hunting d6 mice, attempting to trade
4. 2d6 Deeritz, herding d6 mice, travelling together
5. Run an Adventure seed: strange table
6. d6 Lost Battlemice, on clandestine mission
