# Factions

Factions are large forces outside the control of the players who help create a feeling of a living world.

## Goal progression

Between each session, each faction will attempt to advance their current goal.

For each faction, roll d6, add 1d6 to the result.

If a total is 1 or more, the faction is furthered by the goal. Subtract 1 from the goal for each of that faction's relevant resources.

- If the final result is 4-5, mark 1 progress.
- If the final result is 6+, mark 2 progress.

## Using factions

Factions act as forces that the players can interact towards their goals. Goals should reflect the factions' values and interests, and can clash towards other factions.

Resources reflect the power and means of each faction. Each faction has 2-5 within the scope of the faction.

Resources reflect the power and influence a faction can exert on its competition to represent a goal. Add a new resource to represent their increased power and influence.

If the goal reduces another faction's power, delete one of their resources, and add hints of current goals to your rumour table.

## Player interference

As the players interact with factions, show steps of the current faction goals they are working towards.

Players should have opportunities to interfere with the faction's goals.

Factions of players and players may interfere with the faction's two goals.

If the players successfully pursue, interfere in completion of a goal, mark or erase 1-3 progress in the scale of the goal.

If players directly affect a faction and significantly affect the state, this may require you to delete or re-write a faction's resource.

As factions complete goals, update your map to show warning and warning power, and add hints of current goals to your rumour table.
