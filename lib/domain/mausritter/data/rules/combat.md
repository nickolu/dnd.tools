# Core Rules

## Saves

**Overcome:** Roll under your STR, DEX or WIL.

**Advantage:** When making a Save from a strong position, roll 2d20 and take the lowest result.

**Disadvantage:** When making a Save from a weakened position, roll 2d20 and take the highest result.

## Combat

If in doubt about who goes first, each PC must make a DEX Save to act before their enemies.

**Attacks:** On your turn, roll your weapon's damage die. Subtract the target's armour, then deal the remaining damage to the opponent's HP.

**Enhanced:** When attacking from a strong position, roll d12 for damage.

**Impaired:** When attacking from a weak position, roll d4 for damage.

When an attack reduces a target's HP to 0, the remaining damage is dealt to STR. The target must then make a STR Save to avoid **critical damage.** On a fail, the target is incapacitated and will die without treatment.

**Morale:** Enemies must pass a WIL Save to avoid fleeing when they take their first casualty and again when they lose half their number.

**Usage:** Item: roll d6. On a 1-3, the item is used up. On a 4-6, it remains. Usage die applies to each weapon, armour and spell.

## Rest

**Short:** A brief rest. Restore all HP.

**Long:** A full night's rest in a safe location. Recover an attribute score by 1d6 pips up to its maximum. If full, restore a Condition if listed.

**Full:** A week's rest in a safe location. Fully restore all HP and attribute scores.

## Magic

Invest 1-3 Power (up to number of current dice).

For each Power invested, roll d6. The spell effect happens, scaling with [SUM] or [DICE].

For each die that shows a 6, take WIL damage.

Make a WIL Save, with a failed Condition if failed.

Whenever every 6 is rolled, take d6 WIL damage.
